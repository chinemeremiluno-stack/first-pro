FALCON STRIKE

Hold the runway heading and push the throttle up to take off. Use the left stick to pitch/roll, the throttle slider to control speed. The crosshair turns red and "GUN SOLUTION" appears when you're aligned close enough to land bullets. Hold an enemy in the lock cone to arm a missile, then fire it for a one-shot kill.

Pick a MISSION for objectives and bonus XP, or choose SURVIVAL for endless waves. Earn XP and unlock achievements.

🛡 SURVIVAL
Endless enemy waves. Survive as long as possible. Difficulty scales over time.
✈ ESCORT MISSION
Protect the friendly transport as it flies to safety. Keep it alive for 90 seconds.
💥 DESTROY THE BASE
Fly to the enemy installation and destroy all 5 radar towers. Watch for AA fire.
🤝 PROTECT ALLIES
Your wingmen are under attack. Destroy all enemies before they lose all 3 ally planes.
⏱ TIMED ASSAULT
Destroy 10 enemies within 2 minutes. Every second counts. No mercy.
